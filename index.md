<!-- Feature grid -->
<section class="features" aria-label="Features">
  <p class="features-section-title">What Dumpr does</p>
  <div class="features-grid">

    <div class="card feature-card">
      <span class="feature-icon" aria-hidden="true">🗣️</span>
      <h3>Speak, don't type</h3>
      <p>Tap once. Ramble freely — errands, ideas, appointments, half-formed thoughts. Dumpr records continuously, even through pauses, so you never lose your train of thought.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card feature-card">
      <span class="feature-icon" aria-hidden="true">✂️</span>
      <h3>Automatic segmentation</h3>
      <p>Your stream of consciousness is split into discrete items. "Buy milk, lunch with Ali at 1 tomorrow, call the dentist Friday, that idea about modular routing" becomes four ready-to-review items.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card feature-card">
      <span class="feature-icon" aria-hidden="true">🏷️</span>
      <h3>Three-class routing</h3>
      <p>Each item is classified as a <strong>task</strong>, a <strong>calendar event</strong>, or a <strong>note</strong> — and sent to the right place: Reminders, Calendar, or Notes.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card feature-card">
      <span class="feature-icon" aria-hidden="true">🔒</span>
      <h3>Entirely on-device</h3>
      <p>Speech recognition, classification, and routing all run on your iPhone. No audio, no transcripts, and no captures ever leave your phone. No account. No Dumpr server.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card feature-card">
      <span class="feature-icon" aria-hidden="true">✅</span>
      <h3>Always reviewable</h3>
      <p>Nothing routes until you confirm. Edit text, flip categories, exclude items, or split a combined row before anything touches your calendar or reminders.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="card feature-card">
      <span class="feature-icon" aria-hidden="true">💩</span>
      <h3>Dump on your friends</h3>
      <p>Share a list with someone who has Dumpr — pick your tone (cheeky or serious) and send a link. They open it straight in the app, ready to route. No server involved; the whole payload travels in the link.</p>
    </div>

  </div>
</section>
